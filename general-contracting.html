Homes & Additions

We Build the Whole Thing

Metal buildings are what we build most, but they are not all we build. We take on ground-up homes, additions onto existing houses, and commercial structures, running the job as the general contractor from permit to punch list.

That means one number to call. We schedule the trades, keep the job moving, handle the inspections, and answer for it when something needs sorting. You are not refereeing six contractors and hoping their calendars agree.

No project is too big. No detail is too small.

Talk About Your Build
One Call Every Trade

Licensed and Insured Trades, Coordinated Start to Finish

Bar D is locally owned and family operated, and the work is executed through fully licensed and insured subcontractors that we coordinate as the general contractor. Texas does not license general contractors at the state level, but the trades that Texas does license, including electrical, plumbing, and HVAC, are handled by licensed professionals. Ask us for current certificates on your job and we will get them to you.

Building on Raw Land

Everything the Ground Needs First

Building on undeveloped acreage is a different job than building on a finished lot. There is no driveway, no pad, no utilities, and often no clear idea of where the water goes when it rains.

Because we already do the clearing, the dirt work, the concrete, and the fencing, that whole first phase is us. By the time framing starts, the ground has been handled by the same crew that is going to stand behind the finished building.
