Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Bar D Construction & Land Services wants this website to work for everyone, including people using screen readers, keyboard navigation, screen magnification, or reduced-motion settings.

Our Target

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. That is the standard most commonly referenced for public websites in the United States.

What We Have Built In

  • Semantic HTML with a single main landmark and a logical heading order on every page
  • Descriptive alternative text on content images
  • Labels tied to every form field, with inline error messages rather than pop-up alerts
  • Keyboard access to the menu, the accordions, and the carousel controls
  • Visible focus states and color contrast checked against the AA thresholds
  • Full support for the reduced-motion setting, which stops the scrolling banner, the auto-advancing gallery, and the scroll animations
  • Layouts that reflow down to small phone widths without horizontal scrolling

Where We Know We Are Not Perfect

This is an ongoing effort, not a finished box to check. Third-party components and future content updates can introduce problems we have not caught. If something on this site is hard to use, we want to hear about it, and we will fix it.

Getting the Information Another Way

If any part of this site prevents you from getting what you need, call us at (512) 525-6475. We will happily walk you through our services, take your project details, and give you a free quote over the phone. No part of doing business with Bar D requires you to use this website.

Feedback

Tell us what you ran into, what page you were on, and what device or assistive technology you were using. Call (512) 525-6475 or use the contact form. We take these reports seriously and we will get back to you.
