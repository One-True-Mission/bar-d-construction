Featured Build

The Two-Container Retreat

It starts as two steel boxes sitting on a bluff and ends as a place you can sleep in the night it is finished. Cutting the openings is the easy part. What makes it livable is everything after: structure, framing, insulation, wiring, plumbing, and a finish that does not read as industrial unless you want it to.

  1. 01
    Set on Site Ground cleared and leveled, both containers set and squared where the build will sit.
  2. 02
    Structure Up Steel roof structure welded across both containers, tying them into one building.
  3. 03
    Cut and Closed In Openings cut and reinforced, doors and windows set, roof panel on, deck framing started.
  4. 04
    Deck and Stain Covered deck built the length of the suites and stained, looking out over the country below.
  5. 05
    Finished Sliding doors, trim, lighting, and a covered porch between the two containers.

Guest Quarters

Stay While You Build

Somewhere to live on the land during a ground-up build, that keeps its value as guest space afterward.
