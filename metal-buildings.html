The Detail Work

What Separates a Build From a Kit

These are the things we get asked about most, and they are the things that decide whether a building still looks right in ten years.

Austin Stone Wainscot

Stone wainscot around the base with matching trim, so a working shop reads finished from the driveway instead of looking like bare panel.

Roll-Up Doors & Openings

Roll-up doors sized and framed for what actually has to fit through them. Tractors, trailers, and equipment, not a guess off a catalog.

Trim & Gutters

Full trim package and gutters run as part of the build, so water leaves the structure where you want it instead of cutting a trench beside it.

Crow's Foot Trusses

Carports and covered spans built with crow's foot trusses on 6x6 columns. Real framing, sized for Texas wind, not a bolt-together carport.

Tie-Ins to Existing Structures

When a cover ties into a house, we fasten roof jacks to the top plate and flash it properly. A clean tie-in is where most add-ons leak.

Concrete in the Same Build

Slab, apron, and driveway poured by the same crew on the same schedule, formed to drain and finished to match the building.

The Build Sequence

How a Metal Building Comes Together

01

Site Walk and Free Quote

We come look at the land. Access, slope, drainage, utilities, and where the building actually wants to sit. Then you get a written scope and price, at no cost.

02

Clear, Cut, and Prep the Pad

Brush and trees cleared, pad cut to grade, drainage corrected so water moves away from the slab instead of sitting under it.

03

Foundation Poured

Forms set, steel tied, and the slab poured to the size and thickness the building and its use call for. Aprons and driveways get poured on the same schedule.

04

Steel Set and Welded

Columns, trusses, and purlins set and welded on site, then the building gets closed in with panel, trim, gutters, and doors.

05

Trades, Finish, and Walk

Licensed electrical, plumbing, HVAC, and septic coordinated by us. Interior finish-out to whatever level you want, then we walk the building with you and haul off the debris.
