Our Story

From Land Services to Ground-Up Construction

Bar D is owned and run by Ryan Davidson, and it is family operated out of Stephenville. Twelve years in the industry, seven-plus of those running Bar D under our own name.

We started on the land side. Clearing, dirt work, fence line, the things that have to happen before anybody can build anything. Customers kept asking whether we could handle the next piece too, and then the piece after that, and eventually the answer was that we could just do the whole thing. Now we take projects from raw acreage all the way to a finished building.

The name is a cattle brand. A bar next to a D, the way you would burn it into a hide. That is not decoration, it is where we come from, and it is a fair description of how we work: plain, permanent, and ours.
